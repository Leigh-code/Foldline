    Lighting changes the 3D view, images and sequences. The 3D model download carries no lighting, because Stager and other 3D tools light the scene themselves. Even is the flattest light for checking artwork colours.

    Emboss

    A black-and-white image from the same artboard: black is raised. A ready-made normal map also works. Layers named Emboss or Deboss in the dieline SVG are applied automatically.

    Foil / Spot UV

    mm

    Tiles over silver. Transparent parts show silver, and brighter parts shine more.

    A black-and-white mask from the same artboard: black is foiled. A layer named Foil in the dieline SVG is applied automatically, and a colour in its name, such as "Gold foil", sets the foil.

    Clear gloss varnish over the print. A black-and-white mask from the same artboard: black is varnished. A layer named Spot UV or Varnish in the dieline SVG is applied automatically. Where foil and Spot UV overlap, foil wins.

    Saves the 3D view as you see it now.

    Animated sequence

    A numbered PNG sequence in a zip, ready for After Effects, Premiere or Keynote. The camera stays still and frames the whole fold. To make a video on a Mac, unzip it, open QuickTime Player, choose File › Open Image Sequence…, pick the folder, then save the movie.
